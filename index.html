Deckbuilder Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Deckbuilder is an Android app for cataloguing trading cards. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

Deckbuilder is developed by Patrick Fekete, who is responsible for the processing described here. You can reach me at supagamesstudios@gmail.com.

In short

  • There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.
  • Your collection, your scans and your card images stay on your device.
  • The app sends card lookups to Scryfall, a card database, and crash reports to Google.
  • Card scanning and text recognition run on your device. Your scans are not uploaded.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your collection (cards, quantities, decks, owned and wanted status), the images of cards you scan, and card images downloaded from Scryfall. This data stays on your device. I never receive it.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your Google account backup. That backup is handled by Google under its terms, and you can turn it off in your device settings.

Card lookups through Scryfall

The app identifies cards through Scryfall, an independent card database based in the United States. Your device sends these requests directly to Scryfall over an encrypted connection. They are never sent to me. Scryfall receives:

  • what you type when you search or add a card by name, along with the selected language;
  • the set code, collector number and card name read from a scan, but never the scan itself;
  • requests for card images;
  • a one-time request for the cards you already have, after an app update that stores more information about each card;
  • as with any internet request, your IP address and the app's name and version.

These requests are needed to provide the features you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Scryfall's handling of them is covered by the Scryfall privacy policy.

Card scanning and text recognition

Scanning uses Google's ML Kit document scanner and text recognition, which are provided through Google Play services. Your card images are processed on your device. The first time you scan, Google Play services may download these components.

ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage information, such as the device model, Android version, image size, processing time and error codes. Google uses it to maintain and improve ML Kit. See ML Kit's data disclosure.

Crash reports

When the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Google through Firebase Crashlytics. A report contains:

  • technical details of the error, such as the stack trace;
  • device details, such as the model, Android version and available memory, and whether the app was in the foreground;
  • the app version;
  • random identifiers for the app installation and the session, which are used to group reports and count how many installations a crash affects.

Reports contain no name, email address, location or collection data, and they are not used for advertising. I use them only to find and fix errors, which is my legitimate interest in keeping the app working (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google keeps crash reports for 90 days. See Firebase's privacy information.

Exporting and importing

When you export a deck list or your collection, the file goes only to the location you choose in Android's file picker. When you import, the app reads only the file you pick.

Permissions

The app requests only internet and network status access. It has no access to your camera, photos, location or contacts. The scanner runs in Google Play services and hands the app only the card image you confirm.

Transfers outside the EU

Google and Scryfall may process data in the United States. Google does this under the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

How long data is kept

  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Crash reports are deleted by Google after 90 days.
  • Scryfall keeps its request logs according to its own policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, such as the one in your country. To exercise any of these rights, email me. Crash reports carry no name or contact details, so I can only find yours with your help, for example the date and time of the crash.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
